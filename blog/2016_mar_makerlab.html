My Experience as a MakerLab Guru

Summary

After a few months working as an assistant at the UIUC MakerLab, I was hired as a Guru. This position gave me hands-on experience managing a 3D printing lab, leading workshops, and creating new opportunities for students to learn about 3D printing. I gained technical skills, communication experience, and a chance to participate in a national competition.

Key Contributions

  • Maintained the Ultimaker 3D printers to ensure they ran efficiently for students’ projects, including regular upkeep and troubleshooting.
  • Oversaw the processing of 3D print jobs for students, managing the entire process from scheduling time slots to slicing and post-print cleanup.
  • Developed and led workshops, creating new content for groups of up to twenty people, which helped teach 3D printing techniques and broaden access to the MakerLab’s resources.
  • Actively managed sales and general lab operations, ensuring a smooth workflow and a welcoming atmosphere for students.

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was identifying and addressing technical and communication issues as they arose. From handling last-minute technical glitches to explaining complex printing techniques to beginners, this role required fast thinking and creative problem-solving. I learned to improvise and communicate effectively, which made each day at the lab rewarding and engaging.

Key Achievement

Through my role at the MakerLab, I had the opportunity to participate in the national Make48 competition, a unique event where teams create a product prototype within 48 hours. Our team won, and it was a thrilling experience that showcased the collaborative and innovative spirit fostered at the MakerLab.

Technologies Used

  • Ultimaker 3D Printers - General operation, slicing, and maintenance
  • 3D Modeling Software - Tools for creating printable designs
  • Workshop Design & Facilitation - Organizing and presenting 3D printing workshops for student groups
